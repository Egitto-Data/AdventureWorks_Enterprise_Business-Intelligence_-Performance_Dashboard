# AdventureWorks Enterprise Business Intelligence & Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This repository contains a full-stack Power BI analytics solution built on top of the AdventureWorks enterprise dataset. The project ingests raw transaction, customer, product, and territory data, transforms it through Power Query, connects it in a snowflake schema model, and presents dynamic KPIs and reports to support executive decision-making, sales performance analysis, customer segmentation, and product inventory management.

---

## Data Pipeline & Modeling Architecture

### 1. Data Ingestion & Transformation Process
* **Sales Data Folder Extraction:** Ingests and combines multiple monthly transaction CSV files dynamically from a directory using M custom functions.
* **Data Cleansing & Standardization:**
  * Cleaned customer name fields by converting prefixes, first names, and last names to proper capital case and concatenating them into `Full Name`.
  * Extracted customer email domains to classify user contact properties.
  * Corrected data types across dates, currencies, and whole numbers.
  * Replaced missing values and removed error records across core tables.
* **Calculated Attributes (DAX & M):**
  * Added dynamic date fields to `Calendar Lookup` (Day Name, Start of Week/Month/Quarter/Year, Day of Week, and Weekend vs. Weekday flag).
  * Segmented customers into `Income Level`, `Education Category`, `Parent Status`, and `Customer Priority` flags.
  * Classified products into `Price Points` (High, Mid-Range, Low) and extracted `SKU Category`.

### 2. Data Model (Snowflake Schema)
The data model connects two main Fact tables (`Sales Data` and `Returns Data`) to a normalized set of Dimension tables, including multi-level category hierarchies:

* **Fact Tables:**
  * `Sales Data`: Order dates, transaction quantities, customer keys, product keys, territory keys.
  * `Returns Data`: Return dates, returned quantities, product keys, territory keys.
* **Dimension Lookup Tables:**
  * `Calendar Lookup` $\rightarrow$ linked to `Sales Data[OrderDate]` and `Returns Data[ReturnDate]`.
  * `Customer Lookup` $\rightarrow$ linked to `Sales Data[CustomerKey]`.
  * `Product Lookup` $\rightarrow$ linked to `Sales Data[ProductKey]` and `Returns Data[ProductKey]`.
  * `Territory Lookup` $\rightarrow$ linked to `Sales Data[TerritoryKey]` and `Returns Data[TerritoryKey]`.
  * `Product Subcategories Lookup` $\rightarrow$ linked to `Product Lookup[ProductSubcategoryKey]`.
  * `Product Categories Lookup` $\rightarrow$ linked to `Product Subcategories Lookup[ProductCategoryKey]`.

## Business Metrics & DAX Measures Breakdown

Below is a detailed guide to all DAX measures built into the model, their technical definitions, and how they solve business problems:

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Core Financials
* **`Total Revenue`**: Calculates overall revenue generated by multiplying unit price by order quantity across sales rows.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Provides top-line growth metrics to monitor gross sales generation.
* **`Total Cost`**: Multiplies unit cost by order quantity to compute total production/acquisition costs.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Tracks operating costs directly tied to inventory sold.
* **`Total Profit`**: Subtracts `Total Cost` from `Total Revenue`.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Highlights net margin generation across product lines and operational territories.
* **`Total Orders`**: Counts unique order numbers in sales transactions.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Monitors transactional volume regardless of unit bundle sizes.
* **`Total Customers`**: Distinct count of purchasing customers.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Measures customer base size and active buyer engagement.
* **`Quantity Sold`**: Sums all sold item quantities.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Evaluates overall unit velocity and supply chain requirements.

---

### 2. Return & Operational Efficiency Analysis
* **`Total Returns`**: Total count of returned item records.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Monitors product dissatisfaction or potential quality assurance failures.
* **`Quantity Returned`**: Sum of item quantities returned.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Identifies volume loss due to returns.
* **`Return Rate`**: Ratio of `Quantity Returned` to `Quantity Sold`.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Pinpoints high-risk products or categories experiencing quality issues.
* **`% of All Returns`**: Evaluates filtered returns relative to total dataset returns.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Contextualizes return concentrations to prioritize quality control efforts.
* **`Bike Returns`**, **`Bike Sales`**, **`Bike Return Rate`**: Category-specific tracking for the high-value Bikes category.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Provides dedicated monitoring for the company's highest revenue-generating category.

---

### 3. Customer & Order Behavioral Analytics
* **`Average Revenue Per Customer`**: Ratio of total revenue to unique purchasing customers.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Helps marketing evaluate customer monetary value and target high-value buyer profiles.
* **`New Customers`**: Counts buyers whose initial purchase occurred in the selected month.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Tracks customer acquisition success over time.
* **`Bulk Orders`**: Counts orders containing order quantities greater than 1.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Identifies wholesale or high-volume buyer behaviors.
* **`Weekend Orders`**: Orders placed specifically on Saturdays and Sundays.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Assists operations and marketing in evaluating weekend promotion strategies.
* **`Cohort Performance`**: Evaluates customer retention and transaction activities grouped by initial purchase month.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Analyzes customer lifetime activity patterns and churn trends.
* **`Full Name (Customer Detail)`**, **`Total Orders (Customer Detail)`**, **`Total Revenue (Customer Detail)`**: Dynamic single-selection customer callouts.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Powers drill-through customer report cards without visually cluttering high-level dashboards.

---

### 4. Target Tracking, Forecasting & Trend Analysis
* **`Previous Month Revenue`**, **`Previous Month Orders`**, **`Previous Month Profit`**, **`Previous Month Returns`**: Time-intelligence measures retrieving prior month figures.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Establishes baseline comparisons for period-over-period evaluation.
* **`Revenue Target`**, **`Order Target`**, **`Profit Target`**: Dynamic projections set at 10% above the prior month's performance.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Automatically generates monthly growth targets for sales teams.
* **`Revenue Target Gap`**, **`Order Target Gap`**, **`Profit Target Gap`**: Variance measures subtracting targets from actual performance.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Immediately flags underperforming or overperforming business segments.
* **`10-day Rolling Revenue`**: Moving 10-day sum of total revenue.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Smooths out daily volatility to uncover short-term sales momentum.
* **`90-day Rolling Profit`**: Moving 90-day sum of profit.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Tracks quarterly profit health over rolling windows.
* **`YTD Revenue`**: Cumulative year-to-date revenue.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Monitors progress against annual executive revenue benchmarks.

---

### 5. What-If Scenario Analysis & Visual Formatting
* **`Adjusted Price`**, **`Adjusted Revenue`**, **`Adjusted Profit`**: Dynamic measures driven by the `Price Adjustment (%)` parameter.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Enables leadership to simulate pricing change scenarios (-100% to +100%) and project impacts on overall revenue and profitability.
* **`Revenue KPI Card SVG`**: Native DAX vector graphic render string generating customized visual indicators, color-coded status badges, and dynamic progress bars.
  * *Business Problem Solved:* Delivers custom executive-level visual KPIs directly inside reporting tables.
